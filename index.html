Frequently asked questions

Is the villa rented exclusively?

Yes. LittleKariba is exclusive-use, so the villa and grounds are yours alone for your stay.

How many guests can stay?

Up to 14 guests across seven en-suite bedrooms.

Is food provided?

The villa is self-catering with daily housekeeping. A private chef can be requested. Billing and grocery arrangements are confirmed on enquiry.

What about wildlife?

Kariba is a wildlife area and animals can pass through, including near water. Please follow the guidance you'll be given on arrival, keep children supervised, and don't approach wild animals or the dam edge at night.

Can I pay online?

No. Send an inquiry and we'll confirm availability and the booking process with you directly.
